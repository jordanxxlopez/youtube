## Story and audience

Teo misses a gentle soft-ball catch, then explores a room-size brain simulation with scientist Dani on the intercom to discover how the brain and body cooperate. Four lobe visits collect clues, the cerebellum and body pathways complete the movement team, and three cell guide programs introduce the microscopic team. Teo returns for another gentle try. A successful catch closes the story, while the dialogue makes clear that missing is normal and practice can help.

The episode is for children, with simple labels, a clear mission, restrained humor, and six answerable questions. Watching is sufficient; no movement is required from the viewer. The same story, dialogue, questions, action timing, and sound design serve both versions.
