# Teo — approved design must stay consistent

Before creating, editing, animating, modeling, or depicting Teo, read
`CHARACTER_LOCK.md` and visually inspect both files in `images/`.

- The approved 2D and 3D images define Teo's canonical appearance. They override
  older Teo descriptions, earlier models, loose inspiration, and prompt wording
  when those sources conflict with the approved visuals.
- Preserve his exact identity, facial features, adult proportions, muscular
  build, skin tone, curls, outfit, muted palette, and accessories. Do not
  independently improve, simplify, age, restyle, or redesign him.
- Use the relevant approved image as an actual visual reference for every new
  depiction. A text prompt alone is insufficient to preserve this design.
- Keep the two approved PNG masters unchanged. Put derivatives in separate
  files. Never overwrite the masters, even when a derivative looks similar.
- Change only the pose, action, framing, or setting requested for the current
  work; do not treat a new scene as permission to change his design.
- Movement is allowed and expected: his mouth moves when he speaks and his body
  moves naturally when he walks or acts. Motion never changes his design. See
  "Movement and animation" in `CHARACTER_LOCK.md`.
- If references cannot be accessed or a requested change would alter his
  canonical design, identify the issue before substituting anything. A new
  canonical design requires the user's explicit approval.
- Compare completed work against both references before treating it as final.
  For identical artwork, reuse the approved PNG; a new generation cannot
  guarantee pixel-identical output.

The lock is a production rule, not a technical access restriction. Integrity
hashes in `SHA256SUMS` and `reference-manifest.json` identify the approved bytes.
